Texture source overlays
=======================

These DDS, Paint.NET and packing files were moved unchanged from `menu/hi/modified`.
They are partial authoring overlays for the separate texture package. They do not
replace the complete baseline required for rebuilding its paired archives. Promotional
artwork lives in the top-level `images` folder instead.

The 2026-09-25 recovery also preserves Hadeon's high/low TPF members and 92 added
DDS entries in the `c2500_h-texbnd-dcx` and `c2500_l-texbnd-dcx` folders. These
belong to the main package's character binders, separately from the menu package.
See [the extraction manifest](../extracted-members.json) for exact ownership,
member metadata and hashes. Nested equipment DDS files stay beside their model
members under `src/models`. Rebuild qualification remains separate from extraction.
