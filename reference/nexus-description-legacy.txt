[center][img]https://i.imgur.com/ncRJMv5.gif[/img][/center][/size][size=5][center][color=#666666]--[/color][color=#efefef] Overview & Features [/color][color=#666666]--[/color][/center][/size][/b][left][b][u][img]https://i.imgur.com/WiiRlNG.png[/img][/u][/b][b][color=#f1c232][size=4]﻿[/size][/color][/b][b][color=#f1c232][size=4]﻿[/size][/color][/b][/left][b][size=4][size=4][color=#cc4125][color=#ff0000][size=4]Nightmare Difficulty[/size][/color]
[color=#f4f4f4][size=3]Enemies have 1.5x HP/FP/SP, defenses, and damage. They have increased perception and engage from a greater distance.
Four tiers of enemies: [size=3]Base (1.5x HP), Elite [size=3](2.25x HP)[/size], Boss [size=3](3x HP)[/size], and Nightmare [size=3](4.5x HP)[/size].
[/size][/size][size=3]Nightmare Bosses are the most powerful in-universe, possessing exceptional resilience and overwhelming might.

[color=#f4f4f4][size=3]Nightmare+: Malenia's attacks heal her for double the amount and cannot be dodged.[/size][/color]
[/size][size=3]Nightmare+: Maliketh's destined death attacks deal immense holy damage and cannot be dodged. Full HP for phase 2.[/size][/color]

[color=#f4f4f4]Nemesis: Enemies have a chance to spawn with red eyes, granting them double HP and damage.[/color]
[color=#f4f4f4]They [size=3]will relentlessly hunt you with murderous intent and [/size][/color][color=#f4f4f4]yield significantly more runes when slain. [/color]

[size=4][size=4][color=#cc4125]Dragon Communion Progression[/color][/size][size=3][size=3][color=#f4f4f4][size=4]
[size=3][size=3]Seize strength great enough to challenge gods. With each heart devoured, your power grows, revealing a path toward Divinity. Such power however demands a terrible price.  [/size][/size][/size][/color][/size][/size][/size]

Ritual of Ancient Dragon Communion[/color][/size]
[size=3][size=3][color=#f4f4f4]Ancient dragons are not so different from their drake kin. Hunt and consume their Ancient Dragon Hearts at the Grand Altar of Dragon Communion to unlock powerful new incantations.[/color]

[size=4][color=#b45f06][size=4]Dragonrot, Frenzied Flame, and Bayle's Cursed Blood Afflictions[/size][/color]
[size=3][size=3][color=#f4f4f4]Consuming dragon hearts causes Dragonrot. At low health, your blood starts to decay. Dragonrot will get progressively worse throughout your playthrough if left untreated. Inheriting the Frenzied Flame Frenzied halts Dragonrot but leaves you prone to bursts of madness. Eating Bayle's Heart will [color=#efefef][size=3]cause your blood to erupt in lightning and magma when near death. All afflictions can be halted by equipping the Fatesever Needle Talisman.[/size][/color][/color][/size][/size][/size][/size][/size]

[/i][color=#cc4125][size=4]Dragon Communion[/size][/color]
[color=#efefef][size=3]For every dragon heart consumed, you gain +1 ARC but will also progress your Dragonrot which[/size][size=3][color=#efefef] rots your blood slowly starting at 10% HP. Dragonrot [/color][/size][size=3]gets worse over time, with each Great Rune acquired, your blood starts rotting at a higher HP threshold: 10% -> 20% -> 30% HP and so on.[/size]
[size=3]Lesser Dragonrot: After consuming several dragon hearts, your blood will rot slowly starting at 10% HP. [/size]
[size=3]Greater Dragonrot: After consuming a large amount of dragon hearts, your blood will rot more rapidly. 
[/size][size=3]You can halt all Dragonrot by inheriting the Frenzied Flame or equipping the Fatesever Needle Talisman.[/size]

[size=3]Bayle's Cursed Blood: Consuming Bayle's heart gives +10 ARC but will cause your blood to erupt in lightning and magma when below 10% HP, killing you instantly. It can be halted by equipping the Fatesever Needle. 

Formless Sigil (Dragon Communion Seal): A sigil that imbues incantations with arcane power. [size=3]After [size=3][color=#efefef]consuming all base game dragon hearts via Dragon Communion [/color][/size]and upgrading your Formless Sigil to +10, [size=3]it automatically upgrades into [size=3]Elderblood Sigil.
[/size][/size][/size]
[/size][size=3]Elderblood Sigil: Greater arcane scaling. New Skill: Sovereign Fury. [size=3][size=3][size=3][size=3]After slaying Bayle, battling Placidusax, and [size=3]consuming all available dragon hearts via Dragon Communion[/size], [size=3]Elderblood Sigil[/size][size=3] automatically upgrades into [size=3]Ascension Sigil. 

[/size][/size][/size][/size][/size][/size][/size][size=3]Ascension Sigil: E[/size][b][size=3]xtraordinary [/size][/b][size=3]arcane scaling. New Skill: Sovereign Ascension. Once you have the [/size][color=#efefef][color=#efefef][size=3]Ascension Sigil and can spark Divinity with Sovereign Ascension, you can now turn your eyes to Maliketh and Destined Death. You may now be a match for him.[/size]

[/size][color=#ffd966][size=4]Frenzied Flame:[/size][/color]
[color=#efefef][size=3]Inheriting the Frenzied Flame will halt Dragonrot but will leave you prone to madness giving you +30% Frenzied Flame damage and -30% Madness resistance.
Any change to your HP will have a small chance of triggering the Frenzied Flame affliction which will advance it to the next stage:
Frenzied Flame Stage 1: Slow madness buildup.
Frenzied Flame Stage 2: Rapid madness buildup.
Frenzied Flame Stage 3: Release all madness and howl in agony, losing 30% HP, 20% FP, and damaging nearby enemies.
[size=3]Using Margit's Shackle can temporarily halt the affliction, while equipping the Fatesever Needle offers a more lasting solution.[size=3]
