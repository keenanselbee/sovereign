[center][color=#CC4125][size=6]Sovereign[/size][/color]
[i]The flame devours as it grants power. The blood remembers every sin.[/i]
[i]To ascend is to suffer, to claim is to forsake. None tread this path unchanged.[/i]
[i]Art thou fated to rise as Sovereign, or be unmade upon the path?[/i][/center]

[line]

[b]Sovereign[/b] reshapes Elden Ring around precise deflection, powerful Oaths, reworked weapons, and dangerous paths to godhood. Turn defense into devastating attacks, draw strength from Great Runes and Dragon Communion, and face the growing influence of [b]Nemesis, the Blood Star[/b].

Power comes at a price. Dragon Communion brings afflictions, blood abilities spend your own health, and stronger blessings ask you to change how you fight. Learn their strengths, work around their costs, and build a Tarnished who can stand against the gods.

[heading][color=#CC4125][size=5]Quick Start Guide[/size][/color][/heading]

[b][color=#ffffff]1. Learn the Core Controls[/color][/b]

The guide uses action names so the same instructions work with a controller or keyboard. Use your assigned buttons or keys; [b]Action[/b] means interact.

[b]Recommended controller setup:[/b] We highly recommend swapping the Block and Skill bindings in the game's controller settings: [b]Block on LT and Skill on LB[/b] (PlayStation: [b]Block on L2 and Skill on L1[/b]). The Xbox examples below use this layout.

[list]
[*][b]Crouch attack:[/b] Press Light Attack while crouching. e.g. [i]left stick, then RB.[/i][/*]
[*][b]Weapon ultimate:[/b] Press Block and Heavy Attack together when your ultimate is ready. e.g. [i]LT + RT.[/i][/*]
[*][b]Quick cast your first spell:[/b] Hold Action and press Skill. e.g. [i]Y + LB.[/i][/*]
[*][b]Quick cast your second spell:[/b] Hold Action and press Light Attack. e.g. [i]Y + RB.[/i][/*]
[*][b]Activate your Oath:[/b] Hold Action and press Crouch when your Oath is ready. e.g. [i]Y + left stick.[/i][/*]
[/list]

Tap block just before an attack connects. Practice against an enemy whose timing you know, and watch your stamina as well as your health. Deflection is a core part of Sovereign's combat, available without drinking the Deflecting Hardtear.

[b][color=#ffffff]2. Learn Your Weapon's Full Moveset[/color][/b]

With a signature weapon equipped, crouch and press Light Attack. Try this both one-handed and two-handed, since the attacks can differ.

Kills and deflects charge your ultimate. When the readiness icon appears, press Block and Heavy Attack together. The timing window is brief, so do not hold Block first and wait before attacking. Some weapons also need their weapon buff active. The ultimate is a separate move from your ordinary weapon skill.

[b][color=#ffffff]3. Set Up Quick Spell Casting[/color][/b]

At a Site of Grace, put two spells you want close at hand in your first two memory slots. Equip a staff or sacred seal that can cast them. Hold Action and press Skill to cast the first spell, or Light Attack to cast the second, without cycling through your spell list.

The shortcuts use the normal casting requirements and costs. They return to your previously selected spell after choosing the quick cast, so you can keep another spell selected for regular casting. If you equip casting tools in both hands, the shortcut uses the left-hand one; make sure it can cast your chosen spells.

[b][color=#ffffff]4. Find and Use an Oath[/color][/b]

Radagon's and Marika's Scarseals grant access to Order; their Soreseals grant Golden Order. Radagon's Scarseal can be earned at the Weeping Evergaol. Equip the relevant seal, then hold Action and press Crouch to use your Oath when it is ready. These blessings are part of your expected toolkit.

[b][color=#ffffff]5. Understand the Shrine Before Breaking the Crystal[/color][/b]

Hadeon guards the shrine of Nemesis in the Stranded Graveyard. Defeating him opens the shrine's rite and gives you a choice about the crystal. You do not need to destroy it to obtain the Aberrant Bindseal. Destroying the crystal releases Nemesis and activates Nightmare Mode for that journey.

[b][color=#ffffff]6. Read the Cost of New Power[/color][/b]

Dragon Communion, Rapture, Fury, and other powerful abilities have different costs and conditions. A blood ability may replace holy blessings; an affliction may become more dangerous at low health. Build around the combination you want to use.

[line]

[heading][color=#CC4125][size=5]Difficulty[/size][/color][/heading]

[b]Sovereign is challenging, even outside Nightmare Mode.[/b] It gives you powerful tools to meet that challenge: deflection, Oaths, weapon ultimates, and strong blessings. Learning to use them is part of the intended experience.

[b]For the most forgiving path, serve Nemesis.[/b] Equipping the Blood Star's Aberrant Bindseal grants protective blessings, access to Sin and Thorn Ward, and protection from the entity's influence. It also suppresses Nightmare Mode if you have released Nemesis. Enemies retain their baseline difficulty increases, but the Blood Star's aid makes the journey easier.

[b]Enemy Strength[/b]

The table includes Sovereign's baseline modifier and each assigned enemy tier. Guard damage means stamina damage against your guard; stance and buildup columns show how much the enemy receives.

[code]
| Enemy tier | Health | Attack power | Guard damage | Stance taken | Buildup taken |
|------------|--------|--------------|--------------|--------------|---------------|
| Ordinary   | x1.5   | x1.5         | x1.5         | 75%          | 100%          |
| Elite      | x2.25  | x1.5         | x2           | 66.6%        | 80%           |
| Boss       | x3     | x1.5         | x2.5         | 58.3%        | 65%           |
| God        | x4.5   | x1.5         | x3           | 50%          | 50%           |
[/code]

Lower stance damage and status buildup received make enemies harder to stagger or afflict. Enemies also normally gain [b]50% higher flat defense[/b] and increased sight and hearing.

These are modifier totals, not exact comparisons with every vanilla enemy. Individual enemy changes, regional scaling, and journey scaling also apply. Attack power and flat defense do not translate directly into equivalent percentages of final damage.

[b]The Growing Threat of Nemesis[/b]

Red-eyed Nemesis enemies gain [b]another x2 health, attack power, and guard stamina damage[/b], receive [b]half as much stance damage[/b] and [b]65% as much status buildup[/b], and award [b]x3 runes[/b].

[b]Sin Eclipses[/b]

After your first Great Rune, the Blood Star can trigger Sin Eclipses that empower eligible enemies and temporarily prevent fast travel. These onslaughts become more frequent as you collect Great Runes, even while Nemesis remains imprisoned.

Defeat enemies during an eclipse to earn Darklight Shards and, with enough kills, Darklight Arcs when it ends. Serving Nemesis protects you from these onslaughts.

[b]Optional Nightmare Mode[/b]

Shatter the crystal imprisoning Nemesis to release the Blood Star and activate [b]Nightmare Mode[/b] for your current journey. This keeps the Sin Eclipse active, exposing eligible enemies to Nemesis enhancement. The lasting eclipse does not use the ordinary timed onslaught's reward cycle. Resting or reloading does not undo the release; a new journey gives you a fresh Hadeon and crystal choice.

After defeating Hadeon, you can serve Nemesis before or after release. Equip the Blood Star's [b]Aberrant Bindseal[/b] to gain its protection and suppress Nightmare Mode. Removing it restores the mode if Nemesis has been freed. The seal does not remove the baseline enemy modifiers.

[heading][color=#CC4125][size=5]Combat and Controls[/size][/color][/heading]

[b]Deflection[/b]

Deflect reactions vary with your weapon, hand position, and the incoming attack. See Quick Start for timing and controls.

[b]Weapon Ultimates[/b]

Weapons share an ultimate meter. Some require an active weapon buff, including Godslayer's Greatsword and Fallingstar Obliterator; see Signature Weapons for their individual requirements.

[b]Crouch Attacks and Movement[/b]

Sovereign's signature weapons have custom crouch attacks, including distinct one-handed and two-handed moves. Several attacks also change their travel distance when held, letting you adjust how far you commit. Falling attacks gain additional power with height, while some equipment and protective effects change which falls you can survive.

[b]Endure and Blasphemous Claw[/b]

The stationary dodge action can use Endure and, once acquired, Blasphemous Claw. Endure helps you stand through an incoming attack; the Claw adds a timed defensive answer. Their available actions depend on your unlocks, state, and resources.

[b]More Control Shortcuts[/b]

The Quick Start Guide above covers crouch attacks, ultimates, quick spell casting, and Oaths. Two more shortcuts keep useful tools close at hand:

[list]
[*][b]Toggle your lantern:[/b] Hold Action and press Block, once you own a lantern. e.g. [i]Y + LT.[/i][/*]
[*][b]Use Margit's Shackle:[/b] Hold Action and press Dodge, once you have acquired it. e.g. [i]Y + B.[/i][/*]
[/list]

The lantern shortcut replaces the left-hand two-hand shortcut in the default setup. The mod also provides quick access to acquired shackles and gestures, reducing trips through your item and gesture menus.

[heading][color=#CC4125][size=5]Oaths and Allegiance[/size][/color][/heading]

Oaths are powerful tools for surviving Sovereign's harder encounters. The seal you equip determines which Oath you can call upon. Oath readiness has its own cooldown; a blessing can last longer than the wait before your next activation.

[code]
| Oath         | Required equipment             | Main benefit                     |
|--------------|--------------------------------|----------------------------------|
| Order        | Radagon's or Marika's Scarseal | Golden Vow and healing over time |
| Golden Order | Radagon's or Marika's Soreseal | Stronger healing with Golden Vow |
| Totality     | Perfect Runeseal               | Sacred Ward and counterattacks   |
| Sin          | Aberrant Bindseal              | Thorn Ward and Nemesis's favor   |
[/code]

[b]Order and Golden Order[/b]

Order combines Golden Vow with Blessing's Boon. Golden Order upgrades the healing to Blessing of the Erdtree. The seals also have their own equipment bonuses and holy-damage tradeoffs, so choose one for the whole build rather than only its Oath.

[b]Totality and Sacred Ward[/b]

Tell Goldmask that Radagon is Marika to receive the Perfect Runeseal and its gesture. Totality is a defensive stance that answers attacks with Wrath and Sacred Ward. Your stamina matters; the stance is not permission to ignore every attack indefinitely.

Sacred Ward is a strong protective bubble. Its protection can be spent by taking damage, and the reactive Totality stance can renew it. The bubble and the stance are related, but simply having the bubble does not mean you are holding Totality.

[b]Sin and the Aberrant Bindseal[/b]

After defeating Hadeon, stand near Nemesis at the shrine to learn the [b]Rapture[/b] gesture after a short delay. Perform Rapture there and let the ritual finish to receive the [b]Aberrant Bindseal[/b]. You do not need to break the crystal.

Equip the seal to become a follower and gain Sin, Thorn Ward, and Nemesis's blessings. Removing it consumes the seal; return and repeat the ritual to obtain another. See Difficulty for its effect on Nightmare Mode.

[heading][color=#CC4125][size=5]Nemesis and Hadeon[/size][/color][/heading]

Nemesis, the Blood Star, aids you against the guardian of its prison.

[b]Hadeon, Guardian of the Shrine[/b]

Face Hadeon in the Stranded Graveyard. During the fight, Nemesis helps at clear points in his health bar:

[code]
| Hadeon's remaining health | Nemesis's help                              |
|---------------------------|---------------------------------------------|
| 75%                       | Fully heal the player                       |
| 50%                       | Grant Thorn Ward                            |
| 25%                       | A shriek removes 10% of Hadeon's maximum HP |
[/code]

Each gift occurs once per attempt. If Hadeon falls off the arena and is returned, he loses another 5% of his maximum health. Ordinary retreat and reset teleports do not cause that damage.

[b]Thorns Reward Your Deflects[/b]

During Hadeon's fight, deflecting with Thorn Ward releases a short-range thorn burst that damages him without costing health or ward charges. Bursts have a half-second cooldown. Taking damage still uses the ward's normal protection and retaliation. This deflect effect applies only to Hadeon's encounter.

[b]Victory and the Shrine[/b]

Defeating Hadeon awards Erdtree's Favor +3, unlocks the shrine's rite, and reveals the red crystal. The Aberrant Bindseal comes from the separate Rapture ritual described under Oaths and Allegiance.

[heading][color=#CC4125][size=5]Dragon Communion and Afflictions[/size][/color][/heading]

Hunt dragons, learn their incantations, and pursue ancient communion. Your growing command of dragon magic opens new powers, but it also changes the dangers carried in your own blood.

Ancient Dragon Hearts and the Grand Altar of Dragon Communion extend the hunt into more powerful magic. The Profane Tomes below unlock ancient communion and the crafting path to its new catalysts.

[b]Dragonrot[/b]

Dragon Communion brings Dragonrot, an affliction that becomes dangerous when your health is low. Further communion and Great Rune progression can make its effects more severe. Treat low health as a risk to manage, especially when combining dragon powers with abilities that spend your own health.

[b]The Frenzied Flame[/b]

Inheriting the Frenzied Flame suppresses Dragonrot but introduces its own madness affliction. It can build through increasingly dangerous stages and erupt in a damaging outburst. Margit's Shackle can suppress the affliction temporarily.

[b]Bayle's Cursed Blood[/b]

Bayle's power carries a separate curse, threatening a violent eruption when you are near death. It deserves particular care in builds that deliberately fight at low health.

[b]Fatesever Needle[/b]

Equipping the Fatesever Needle suppresses these afflictions. That protection depends on the talisman: it does not mean the underlying curses have been permanently removed.

[line]

[heading][color=#CC4125][size=5]Profane Tomes and Forbidden Knowledge[/size][/color][/heading]

Two Profane Tomes preserve Magus Gyre's research into draconic transfiguration. Finding them unlocks knowledge and crafting recipes; they do not need to be given to an incantation teacher.

[b]Profane Tome [1][/b]

Gyre's first tome hints at the link between Dragonrot, the Frenzied Flame, and unalloyed gold amid a storm. It unlocks Ancient Dragon Communion at the communion altars and the recipe for the [b]Elderblood Communion Seal[/b].

[b]Where to find it:[/b] In the isolated part of Crumbling Farum Azula reached through the Four Belfries' Crumbling Lands waygate, replacing the base Pearldrake Talisman pickup.
[b]Recipe:[/b] Dragon Communion Seal [b]+10[/b] and one Ancient Dragon Heart.

[b]Profane Tome [2][/b]

The second tome describes a crimson cosmos infused with Elderblood, a plan to draw upon the breath of gods, and a mysterious Gate. It unlocks the recipe for the [b]Staff of the Sovereign[/b].

[b]Where to find it:[/b] In Farum Azula's hall between Crumbling Beast Grave Depths and Tempest-Facing Balcony, replacing the Ancient Dragon Prayerbook pickup.
[b]Recipe:[/b] Elderblood Communion Seal and Staff of the Great Beyond.

Crafting the Staff of the Sovereign also unlocks [b]Elderblood Greatbow[/b] in the ancient communion offerings. Finding the second tome alone does not unlock the spell.

[heading][color=#CC4125][size=5]Great Runes and Divinity[/size][/color][/heading]

[b]Great Rune Passives[/b]

Great Runes contribute passive benefits alongside their active effects. They also affect Nemesis encounters and Dragonrot. Your victories can make you stronger while changing the dangers around you.

[code]
| Great Rune | Passive benefit                              |
|------------|----------------------------------------------|
| Godrick    | +1 to all attributes                         |
| Rennala    | +5 Mind                                      |
| Radahn     | +20% maximum equip load                      |
| Morgott    | +5 Faith                                     |
| Rykard     | Additional benefits from defeating enemies   |
| Mohg       | Benefits tied to blood loss                  |
| Malenia    | Changes to attack stamina costs and recovery |
[/code]

[b]Sovereign Fury[/b]

Fury draws on ancient dragon power to increase your resources and strengthen movement, defense, and lightning-based combat. Its casting cost temporarily reduces maximum HP, FP, and stamina. Repeated use is a choice about how much power you need and how much of that cost you can afford.

[b]Soulflame Divinity[/b]

The Staff of the Sovereign's airborne Fury, the Entwining Umbilical Cord, and very high health open the way to Divinity. Repeated airborne casts are dangerous, so forcing the transformation is a risk of its own.

While you avoid damage, Divinity stops many of your buff timers from counting down. It also grants rapid HP, FP, and stamina recovery and lets you move between actions more quickly, rewarding careful play with lasting power.

Get hit, and those buffs begin counting down from where they stopped. Oath and ultimate cooldowns continue normally.

Seek the Entwining Umbilical Cord through the Gloam-Eyed Queen's Grave encounter. The Cord is also an offensive talisman in its own right.

[line]

[heading][color=#CC4125][size=5]Blood Power[/size][/color][/heading]

[b]Rapture and Bloodflame Blasphemy[/b]

Rapture and Mohg's Shackle turn your own blood into power. They support a bloodflame playstyle built around spending health, striking nearby enemies, and drawing on powerful attack and recovery buffs.

Bloodflame Blasphemy replaces holy blessings rather than stacking freely with all of them. Its death-rescue effect can consume the blood buff to save you in an explosion of bloodflame. That rescue is part of the buff, not a permanent extra life.

[b]Supremacy[/b]

Mohgwyn's Sacred Spear's ultimate can grant Supremacy, empowering you through a blood ritual. Its blessing can be spent to save you from death, and it replaces certain other blessings. Choose your supporting buffs with those limits in mind.

[heading][color=#CC4125][size=5]Signature Weapons[/size][/color][/heading]

These weapons offer different ways to fight, not simply larger numbers. Their skills prepare or strengthen their movesets, while crouch attacks and ultimates give you additional ways to spend an opening.

[b][color=#ffffff]Stormblessed Zweihander[/color][/b]

A mobile wind weapon built around sweeping attacks and forward movement.
[b]Skill:[/b] Stormblessed Blade calls the wind into your weapon.
[b]Crouch attacks:[/b] Gale Sweep gives the one-handed weapon a low spinning strike; Skyward Annihilator turns the two-handed attack into a rising blow.
[b]Ultimate:[/b] Whirlwind Slash delivers a broad wind-driven assault.

[b][color=#ffffff]Blasphemous Blade[/color][/b]

A bloodflame weapon that trades health for growing power.
[b]Skill:[/b] Ravenous Bloodflame builds stacks that change the strength and appearance of its attacks.
[b]Crouch attacks:[/b] Furious Thrust lunges forward; Bloodrend commits to a somersaulting strike with bloodflame effects.
[b]Ultimate:[/b] Savage Bloodrend turns stored bloodflame and your current health into a larger burst.

[b][color=#ffffff]Godslayer's Greatsword[/color][/b]

A weapon built around maintaining Black Flame and making the most of brief Soulflame windows.
[b]Skill:[/b] Soulflame Heresy brings together the blade's Black Flame, Soulflame, and Heresy effects.
[b]Crouch attacks:[/b] God Hunt sends out a shockwave whose behavior depends on the active flame.
[b]Ultimate:[/b] Queen's Black Flame delivers a sweeping attack with a follow-up. Its stronger actions depend on preparing the weapon with the right buff.

[b][color=#ffffff]Maliketh's Black Blade[/color][/b]

Destined Death becomes a sustained weapon state, with a stronger awakened opening.
[b]Skill:[/b] Awaken the blade and bring Destined Death into its attacks.
[b]Crouch attacks:[/b] Mortal Thrust drives forward, while Mortal Rend adds a spinning attack and projectile.
[b]Ultimate:[/b] Deliver Destined Death plunges the blade down and unleashes its wider attack. Active weapon effects change what its attacks can deliver.

[b][color=#ffffff]Mohgwyn's Sacred Spear[/color][/b]

A blood ritual weapon whose ultimate changes the fight beyond the initial attack.
[b]Combat role:[/b] Use its ritual and blood effects to pressure enemies while maintaining your own resources.
[b]Ultimate:[/b] The ritual can grant Supremacy, carrying its attack, recovery, and blood-rescue effects into the following fight.

[b][color=#ffffff]Fallingstar Obliterator[/color][/b]

A gravity weapon earned through Hewg, with a prepared beam attack at the center of its moveset.
[b]Acquisition:[/b] Defeat the Godskin Duo and all three base-game Fallingstar Beasts in the current journey, then speak to Hewg. He offers a hint before you qualify. His later dialogue still allows the forging reward, once per journey.
[b]Skill:[/b] Harness Void Eye costs 45 FP and prepares the weapon with a 60-second magic and lightning buff.
[b]Ultimate:[/b] The beam requires Void Eye and refreshes that buff when used. Attempting the ultimate without Void Eye starts the preparation action instead.

[b][color=#ffffff]Great Stars[/color][/b]

A heavy blood-themed weapon built around forceful strikes and committed finishes.
[b]Crouch attacks:[/b] Crush delivers a heavy downward strike; Bloody Smash adds a bloodflame attack to the two-handed moveset.
[b]Ultimate:[/b] Bloodflame Earthshaker combines a ground-shaking attack with a bloodflame burst.

[heading][color=#CC4125][size=5]Magic and Incantations[/size][/color][/heading]

[b]Fire Monk and Godslayer Incantations[/b]

[b]Flame Sling[/b] and [b]Black Flame[/b] reach farther. Their charged projectiles travel faster and produce larger explosions, making charged casts more useful against distant or grouped enemies.

[b]Fire Giant Incantations[/b]

Giant fire gains larger blasts and higher casting costs. [b]Giantsflame Take Thee[/b] reaches farther and explodes over a wider area. Charged [b]Flame, Fall Upon Them[/b] gains longer-reaching projectiles and larger bursts. [b]Flame of the Fell God[/b] moves faster and produces a much larger blast with longer-lasting effects.

[code]
| Incantation             | FP cost          |
|-------------------------|------------------|
| Flame, Fall Upon Them   | 24               |
| Giantsflame Take Thee   | 45               |
| Burn, O Flame!          | 39 / 45 charged  |
| Flame of the Fell God   | 68               |
[/code]

These four incantations draw on a power reserve that changes with your progress:

[list]
[*][b]Before defeating the Fire Giant:[/b] A full reserve doubles their damage. Repeated casting lowers the bonus in 20-percentage-point steps, down to normal damage; one step recovers every 60 seconds. Flame of the Fell God empties the reserve.[/*]
[*][b]After defeating the Fire Giant:[/b] The reserve's damage bonus is lost until you kindle the Forge.[/*]
[*][b]After kindling the Forge:[/b] The full double-damage bonus returns and no longer depletes through casting.[/*]
[/list]

[b]Dragon Cult and Dragonbolt Blessings[/b]

Dragonbolt blessings improve movement, stamina recovery, and resistance to status buildup. Their protection comes with a tradeoff: you take [b]35% more lightning damage[/b].

[code]
| Blessing              | Duration | Movement speed | Status buildup taken |
|-----------------------|----------|----------------|-----------------------|
| Dragonbolt Blessing   | 90 sec   | +5%            | 95%                  |
| Vyke's Dragonbolt     | 90 sec   | +10%           | 90%                  |
| Betrayer's Dragonbolt | 180 sec  | +15%           | 85%                  |
[/code]

[b]Vyke's Dragonbolt[/b] now applies a body blessing without automatically enchanting your weapon. [b]Betrayer's Dragonbolt[/b] also increases your lightning damage against enemies by 25%, blesses nearby allies, and makes nearby enemies more vulnerable to lightning. Its effects on nearby targets last 90 seconds.

[b]Dragon Communion and Ancient Dragon Magic[/b]

Advanced dragon incantations emphasize Arcane. [b]Lansseax's Glaive[/b] requires 48 Arcane and [b]Fortissax's Lightning Spear[/b] requires 52; each costs 9 FP, has five uses, and demands substantial stamina. [b]Placidusax's Ruin[/b] requires 60 Arcane, [b]Bayle's Tyranny[/b] 62, and [b]Bayle's Flame Lightning[/b] 64. Ordinary dragon breaths retain their own casting costs.

[b]Elderblood Greatbow[/b] requires 58 Arcane and costs 40 FP. Fire a chargeable arrow of bloodflame and red lightning that inflicts Dragonrot, reduces healing, and deals especially heavy damage to dragons. Hold the cast to keep the arrow nocked. See Profane Tomes and Forbidden Knowledge for its unlock.

[heading][color=#CC4125][size=5]Equipment and Useful Items[/size][/color][/heading]

[b]Armor That Changes Through Your Journey[/b]

The Scaled armor has a black transformation tied to Rykard. With at least one original Scaled piece equipped, dying during either phase of the fight or defeating Rykard can transform the original pieces you are carrying. Carried quantities and the altered chest piece are preserved; pieces left in storage are not part of that exchange.

This route requires an active Rykard encounter. Equipping the armor after he is already defeated does not recreate it. Other color and equipment effects have their own conditions.

[b]Talismans That Support a Playstyle[/b]

Scarseals, Soreseals, the Perfect Runeseal, and the Aberrant Bindseal provide access to the Oath system. The Entwining Umbilical Cord supports Soulflame and Divinity, while Crusade Insignia rewards kills with a temporary combat buff.

[b]Quick Buffs and Recovery[/b]

Numen's Runes and Rune Arcs let you quickly apply buffs you have access to. You still need the required spells, equipment, or other conditions, and buffs that replace one another cannot all be kept at once. These item-based quick buffs are separate from the two spell-slot shortcuts in the Quick Start Guide.

Starlight Shards support FP recovery, Opaline Bubblesoap provides a protective bubble, and Dragon Flesh and Dragonscale Flesh trade stronger temporary resources against reduced healing.

[b]Shackles as Everyday Tools[/b]

Margit's Shackle becomes a useful darkness and curse-management tool. Mohg's Shackle spends your blood on an area attack and connects to the bloodflame playstyle. Their shortcuts make them easier to reach without replacing the requirements for acquiring them.

[heading][color=#CC4125][size=5]Requirements and Installation[/size][/color][/heading]

Requires [b]Elden Ring on PC[/b]. Shadow of the Erdtree is needed for the DLC content and progression.

[b]Install with Vortex[/b]

[list=1]
[*]Manage [b]Elden Ring[/b] in Vortex.[/*]
[*]Download [b]me3-windows-amd64.zip[/b] from [url=https://github.com/garyttierney/me3/releases/latest]Mod Engine 3's GitHub releases[/url]. Import it with [b]Install From File[/b] and enable it.[/*]
[*]Install and enable [url=https://www.nexusmods.com/eldenring/mods/117]Elden Mod Loader[/url], [b]Sovereign[/b], and [b]Sovereign - Textures[/b] using [b]Mod Manager Download[/b].[/*]
[*]Click [b]Deploy Mods[/b].[/*]
[*]Open Steam, then launch [b]Mod Engine 3[/b] from Vortex's Dashboard.[/*]
[/list]

[b]One-time launcher setup[/b]

On Vortex's Dashboard, add a tool named [b]Mod Engine 3[/b]:
[b]Target:[/b] Select bin\me3.exe inside Elden Ring's Game folder.
[b]Start In:[/b] Select that same Game folder.
[b]Arguments:[/b]
[code]launch --game eldenring --package mod[/code]

[b]Optional: launch from your desktop[/b]

Create a shortcut to Game\bin\me3.exe. In the shortcut's [b]Properties[/b], add [b]launch --game eldenring --package mod[/b] after the quoted executable path in [b]Target[/b], and set [b]Start in[/b] to your Game folder. Open Steam, then use the shortcut to play. You only need Vortex when changing mods; deploy those changes before playing.

Mod Engine 3 automatically blocks connections to FromSoftware's official multiplayer servers. Sovereign includes Script Exposer; Elden Mod Loader loads it automatically.

Back up [b]%APPDATA%\EldenRing[/b] before playing. Disable older Sovereign versions when updating.

[heading][color=#CC4125][size=5]Compatibility[/size][/color][/heading]

Sovereign changes game data, player scripts and animations, events, maps, dialogue, text, equipment, and effects. Another mod that replaces the same files can overwrite part of Sovereign even if the game still launches.

[list]
[*][b]Other gameplay overhauls:[/b] Do not assume they can be combined by changing load order. Conflicting changes need a compatible merge.[/*]
[*][b]Player movesets and animation mods:[/b] Treat these as potential conflicts with Sovereign's deflection, weapon actions, and ultimates.[/*]
[*][b]Map, dialogue, and progression mods:[/b] Check overlaps with the shrine, Grace menus, Hewg, and other altered events.[/*]
[*][b]Texture and interface replacements:[/b] Check which files they replace, including the separate Sovereign - Textures package.[/*]
[/list]

Offline use does not establish multiplayer compatibility. This page does not promise support for a multiplayer overhaul or for every other mod in an existing collection.
